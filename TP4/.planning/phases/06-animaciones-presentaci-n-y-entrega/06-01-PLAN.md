- **PD-120 Four videos, three cases.** Roadmap criterion 1 names three cases: billar con obstáculos (frescas y usadas), varios x0 típicos de 2.2 y sin obstáculos (2.3). The typical x0 of 2.2 are 0.0175, 0.20 and 0.4925 m (Phase 5, `typical_source = auto`): the three obstacle videos are the case "varios x0 típicos", the middle one (x0 = 0.20 m, the favourable zone) is also the characteristic obstacle animation shown first, and `sin_obstaculos` is the 2.3 case. Four uploads, not five.
- **PD-121 Real-time parameters.** N = 100, dt* = 5e-5 s; obstacle videos tf = 40 s with dt2 = 0.04 s (every = 800, 1001 frames, 25 fps = real time, 40 s videos; at x0 = R - r the mean t90 is about 31 s, so most particles are used by the end); no-obstacle video tf = 10 s with dt2 = 0.02 s (every = 400, 501 frames, 25 fps = half speed, so the 0.34 s relaxation lasts about 17 video seconds of the 20). Pitfall 21: frames are subsampled and every frame shows t.
- **PD-122 Representative seed.** Obstacle cases use the seed whose t90 is nearest to the mean of seeds 1 to 10 (cheap pre-pass with `stop="t90"`, no trajectory, tmax 100 s). The pre-pass runs on the local compiler, so it need not match the official WSL runs bit for bit (the chaotic trajectories differ across compilers); the videos illustrate the dynamics, they are not data, and the SUMMARY states it.
- **PD-123 Representative frame.** For obstacle cases the PNG is the first frame at or after the instant of the ceil(N/2)-th conversion (Fu about 0.5), so blue and red both show. For no obstacles it is t = 2 s, after relaxation (all blue by construction).
- **PD-124 Links as data.** One JSON registry plus a generated `links.tex` (tracked, so the Windows machine compiles without Python). `links.py --check` also verifies `links.tex` is up to date. A null URL becomes visible placeholder text in the draft PDF and blocks the final gate (plan 06-04).
- **PD-125 Live deck.** The guide (2.4.8) wants animations embedded during the live talk and absent from the delivered PDF. TP3 solved it with a `vivo` compile mode, page rasterization and GIF overlays in a .pptx; this plan ports that script, deriving the animation order from the deck source instead of a hand-written list.
- **PD-126 Reversibility.** Everything here is regenerable from the frozen engine in a few minutes: no rating beyond reversible.
