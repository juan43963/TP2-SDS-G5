## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion: no CONTEXT.md and no RESEARCH.md for this phase)

- **PD-90 Sweep gate.** One module, `sweep_gate.py`, enforces gates 1, 2 and 5 for every Phase 5 batch: dt* frozen (`require_dt_star`), engine frozen (`freeze.check` and `freeze.check_against_git("HEAD")`), and the official 2.1b session finished. The session evidence is a local `data/timing/session.json` with mode official and status ok, or, when the timing data lives on the other group machine (data/ is gitignored), the tracked README heading that Plan 04-04 writes only after verifying the session. Run or `.partial` directories in `data/timing/` without session.json mean a session is running or was aborted, so the gate blocks regardless of the README. The gate never runs for `--replot` or `--budget`, which launch nothing.
- **PD-91 2.2 grid.** X0_GRID has 11 values in [r, R - r] with both extremes (roadmap: about 10 to 12); 0.4925 is R - r within the engine's 1e-12 tolerance. N in (100, 20); 10 seeds (`engine.make_seeds(10)`, at least 5 required, 10 matches 2.1b and costs well under a minute); tmax = 100 s; init rsa for every N <= 400 (research SUMMARY conflict 5, one disclosed method); no trajectory; stop all_used, which loses no Fu(t) information (after the N-th conversion Fu = 1) and still yields t90 and t100; EVERY = `engine.every_for(DT_STAR, 0.01)` = 200, only to fix the name.
- **PD-92 One run study for 2.2 and 2.4b.** All 2.2 and 2.4b runs live in `data/conversion/`. Plan 05-03 builds its specs with `build_specs` and the same constants, so the N = 100 and N = 20 rows at X0_GRID have identical names and the runner skips them (gate 4). The analysis loads exactly the expected spec names, so extra runs in the directory never leak into 2.2.
- **PD-93 Observables.** t90, t100 and Fu(tmax) per run as in the truths; Fu(t) on `np.arange(0, tmax + FU_DT/2, FU_DT)` with `np.searchsorted(times, t_grid, side="right") / N`; per (N, x0) and t, Fu mean and sample sigma over the realizations.
- **PD-94 Censoring.** The 2.4a rule, by import (`threshold_stats = study_density._threshold_stats`, `k90 = study_density.k90`, `mean_sigma = study_density._mean_sigma`), so 2.2, 2.4a and 2.4b cannot drift apart; study_density.py is not edited. The AN-06 report adds n_censored90 and the Fu(tmax) mean and sigma over the censored runs only. A `none` point is drawn as a hollow upward triangle at y = tmax (its lower bound) with the count `n/n`.
- **PD-95 Optimum and typical x0.** `optimum_along(rows, key, axis)` generalises `study_density.optimum` to any axis (edge: minimum at the first or last complete value; distinct: separated from every complete neighbour by more than the combined sigma). Typical x0 for Fu(t): sorted unique {r, optimum of N = 100 if any, R - r}, plus the X0_GRID value closest to 0.25 when fewer than 3 (2 to 4 required); `--fu-x0` overrides with up to 4 grid values.
- **PD-96 Workers and budget.** `default_workers()` = min(MAX_WORKERS, max(1, cpu_count - 2)). The budget assumes no early stop: worst CPU seconds = sum over pending specs of N * round(tmax / dt) * BUDGET_NS * 1e-9 (18 ns per particle-step, the 2.1b budget constant); wall = CPU / workers. Official 2.2: 220 runs, worst about 400 CPU seconds, well under a minute of wall time with 12 workers.
- **PD-97 Smoke.** Study `conversion_smoke`: x0 (0.0175, 0.25, 0.4925), N (100, 20), seeds (1, 2), tmax 30 s: 12 runs of about a second, with a mix of complete and censored points.
- **PD-98 Q5 default.** Error bars are the sample sigma (ddof 1) everywhere, never sigma/sqrt(n) (REQUIREMENTS Out of Scope, Teórica 0 slide 61); recorded as Q5 in the README.
