## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion: no CONTEXT.md and no RESEARCH.md for this phase)

- **PD-100 Runs.** N = 100, no obstacles, tf = 10 s, dt2 = 0.01 s, 10 seeds, rsa init, trajectories on. Estimates: with n = N / (pi R^2) = 122 m^-2 and a 2D cross-section of 4r, the collision time is about 0.08 s, so relaxation takes well under 1 s and tf = 10 s leaves a window of about 8 s; frames are about 9 KB each, about 9 MB per run, about 95 MB for the official run (the only Phase 5 study that writes trajectories). Cost: about a second per run.
- **PD-101 Ratio and window (declared before any data).** The sampling sigma of the ratio of n speeds drawn from a 2D Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution is 2 / sqrt(n) (delta method on u = v^2, exponential), so with n = N * n_seeds speeds per snapshot the threshold 2 - 3 * 2 / sqrt(n) (1.81 for 10 seeds, 1.70 for the 4 smoke seeds) is reached only once the distribution is close to Maxwell-Boltzmann. Starting the window at 3 t_relax discards the rest of the transient (for an exponential approach the remaining gap is below 1 percent). The window-mean ratio (mean +- sigma over realizations) is printed as a check, not used to choose the window.
- **PD-102 Histograms.** Fixed bins of DV = 0.05 m/s on [0, 5] m/s (100 bins; P(v > 5 m/s) is about 1e-11 at kBT = 0.0125 J), density = counts / (n * DV) so the integral is exactly 1. Per realization: one histogram per snapshot time and one over all snapshots of the window; the figures show the mean and sample sigma over realizations (equal sample counts make the mean equal to the pooled histogram).
- **PD-103 Fit (Teórica 0, AN-09).** One parameter, f_MB(v; kBT) = (m v / kBT) exp(-m v^2 / (2 kBT)) evaluated at the bin centres, E(kBT) summed over all 100 bins (zero-count bins included), grid 1e-3 to 5e-2 J step 1e-5 J (4901 values), argmin; a minimum at either end raises ValueError. kBT_fit comes from the realization-mean stationary histogram; kBT sigma is the sample sigma of the fits of each realization's window histogram. Also printed, not fitted: kBT_kinetic = m <v^2> / 2 over the window (2D equipartition). The fitted value may sit slightly below 0.0125 J because part of the energy is stored in contacts; that is a discussion point, not a bug (Pitfall 12).
- **PD-104 Smoke.** Study `thermal_smoke`: seeds 1 to 4, tf = 4 s (window of about 3 s), same N, dt and bins.
- **PD-105 Figures.** `ratio_vs_t`: realization mean as a thin line with symbol and sigma bar every 0.5 s, dashed lines at 1 and 2 labelled, the window shaded with its start marked. `fv_evolution`: one series per SNAP_TIMES value above 0 plus the stationary average, symbols and sigma bars every 4 bins, x from 0 to 3 m/s; the t = 0 spike drawn as an arrow at v0 annotated "t = 0 s: δ(v − v0)". `fv_stationary_fit`: stationary mean +- sigma as symbols, f_MB at kBT_fit as a solid line and at m v0^2 / 2 as a dashed line. `fit_error_kbt`: E(kBT) over [kBT_fit / 2, 2 kBT_fit] with scientific notation on both axes, a star at the minimum and a dashed vertical line at 0.0125 J.
