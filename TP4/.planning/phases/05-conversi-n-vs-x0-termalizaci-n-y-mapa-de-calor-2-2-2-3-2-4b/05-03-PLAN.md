## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion: no CONTEXT.md and no RESEARCH.md for this phase)

- **PD-110 x0 grid from 2.2 (gate 4).** "Same x0 and seeds" and "grid taken from the 2.2 optimum" are reconciled by keeping every X0_GRID value (so the 2.2 rows are reused whole) and adding the two midpoints around the N = 100 optimum when it is interior. The 2.2 study keeps analysing X0_GRID only, so its published results do not change when the heatmap adds runs; the heatmap rows of N = 100 and N = 20 gain the two refined cells. With 11 base values the heatmap has 11 or 13 columns.
- **PD-111 N grid and init.** (20, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 400): it spans the 2.2 values and densities up to packing fraction 0.47 with one initialisation method, rsa (research SUMMARY conflict 5; above about 400 rsa saturates and 2.4a already covers the dense regime on the lattice). 8 rows, 10 seeds, tmax = 100 s, stop all_used, no trajectory, all through `study_conversion.build_specs` into `data/conversion/`.
- **PD-112 Generation probe.** Placement at the top N is the only step that can fail by construction (RSA jamming, Pitfall 6; obstacles at the wall create cusps, Pitfall 16). The probe runs every (x0, seed) of the final grid at N_top with tf = 20 dt (no physics of interest, only generation), in its own study. A failure whose message contains RSA_FAIL_MARKER lowers N_top by 50, at most N_TOP_TRIES candidates (400, 350, 300); any other failure, or all candidates failing, stops the study. The N grid becomes every base N below N_top plus N_top. Smoke candidates are (100, 50).
- **PD-113 Reuse proof.** Before the batch: the 220 runs of 2.2 must be complete (or the study tells the user to run `make conversion-study`), and `reused: <k>/<n>` is printed. After the analysis: every heatmap cell of N in (20, 100) on X0_GRID must equal the 2.2 summary.csv row in every CELL_COLUMNS field (NaN equal to NaN), printed as `reuse-consistency: ok cells=<k>`; a mismatch stops the study. The engine rewrites `data/conversion/manifest.json` per batch; nothing reads it.
- **PD-114 Budget.** Worst case without early stop at 18 ns per particle-step: the sum of N over the grid is 1470, so 1470 x 2e6 steps x 18 ns = 53 CPU seconds per (x0, seed), about 115 CPU minutes for 13 x0 and 10 seeds minus the reused 220 runs; with 12 workers on 8 cores and 16 threads expect 10 to 20 minutes of wall time, less with early stops. The official launch is long-running and resumable: the executor runs it in the background or relaunches after a tool timeout.
- **PD-115 Figure.** `pcolormesh(x_edges, n_edges, C, shading="flat")` with x and N edges at the midpoints between grid values and half a step beyond the ends; colour map viridis, `Normalize(0, tmax)` shared by t90 and t100, colour bar labelled with `axis_label("Tiempo t90", "s")` (or t100); partial cells drawn with their lower bound on the same scale plus a `Rectangle` overlay hatched "//"; `none` cells grey (0.85) with hatch "xx"; one legend below the axes with proxies for "cota inferior (algunas sin t90)", "ninguna alcanzó t90", "x0 óptimo" and "mínimo en el borde o no distinto"; x `axis_label("Posición de los obstáculos x0", "m")`, y `axis_label("Número de partículas N")` with ticks at the grid values. t90 is the primary map (less censored, Pitfall 10); t100 is drawn too.
- **PD-116 Smoke.** Run study `conversion_smoke` (reuses the 12 smoke runs of 2.2), N (20, 50, 100), x0 from SMOKE_X0 refined around the smoke optimum, seeds (1, 2), tmax 30 s, probe candidates (100, 50).
