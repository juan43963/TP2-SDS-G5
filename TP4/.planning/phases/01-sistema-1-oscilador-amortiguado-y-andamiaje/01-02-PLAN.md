## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion — no CONTEXT.md)

- **PD-04 dt grid** = 5e-3, 2e-3, 1e-3, 5e-4, 2e-4, 1e-4, 5e-5, 2e-5, 1e-5, 5e-6, 2e-6, 1e-6. This is the 1-2-5 log spacing over [1e-6, 1e-2). 1e-2 is excluded because the enunciado asks for dt < 1e-2 s. Every value divides tf = 5 s exactly (checked by the planner: round(5/dt)·dt == 5.0 for all twelve, and %.17g round-trips each value). The grid reaches the round-off regime: Gear-5 flattens near 1e-28 m² for dt ≤ 1e-4, and position-form Verlet turns upward below ≈ 1e-5. So the roadmap's "piso de redondeo explicado en la figura" branch applies. Those points stay in the figure with hollow markers plus an annotation. They are never dropped.
- **PD-05 Fit windows** are the same as tp4_test: [1e-4, 1e-3] for eulerpc, verlet, vverlet and beeman, and [1e-3, 5e-3] for gear5.
- **PD-06** ECM = (1/N)·Σ_{k=1..N}.
- **PD-07 Streaming**. The study reads osc's stdout through a pipe in 200 000-line chunks. One run at dt = 1e-6 is 5·10⁶ rows ≈ 0.3 GB, and 60 runs would be ~3 GB on /mnt/c. Only per-(method, dt) ECM is cached in `data/oscillator/ecm.csv`, and that is what `--replot` reads.
- **PD-12 Round-off criterion**: a point outside its fit window whose ECM is more than 3× the fitted power law is drawn hollow. Planner replica: Verlet at 1e-5 is 3.4× the extrapolation, Gear-5 at 1e-4 is ≫ 10×, and Beeman, VV and Euler PC stay on their power law.
- **PD-13 Style API** (AN-12) is rated `costly` (see Task 1 `<reversibility>`). Every later study script imports it, so its names are fixed here.

  5. feeds rows with k ≥ 1 to an EcmAccumulator (PD-06);
  6. after EOF, requires exactly steps + 1 rows and proc.wait() == 0. Otherwise raises RuntimeError that includes osc's decoded stderr.
