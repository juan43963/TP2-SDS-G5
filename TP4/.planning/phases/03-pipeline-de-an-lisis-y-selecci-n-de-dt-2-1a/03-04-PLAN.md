## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion — no CONTEXT.md for this phase)

- **PD-50 Study parameters.** N = 300, no obstacles, tf = 5 s (about 90 collisions per particle at this density, enough to see drift), common output interval dt2 = 0.01 s (501 frames; every = dt2/dt is an integer for every grid dt), seeds 1 to 5 (the planner measured a factor-2 spread of epsilon between seeds at dt = 5e-5, so five seeds give a usable mean and a sigma), dt grid `(5e-3, 2e-3, 1e-3, 5e-4, 2e-4, 1e-4, 5e-5, 2e-5, 1e-5, 5e-6)`: it starts in the explosion regime (the enunciado asks dt < 1e-2 and Teorica 4 slide 32 expects the explosions to appear), crosses the stability limit near tc/2 and reaches well below the cost-relevant range. The initial state is identical for all dt because the generator stream depends only on the seed.
- **PD-51 Scalar observable and rule (AN-02, DIF-03).** epsilon(dt) = mean over seeds of [mean over the 500 instants t > 0 of |E(t) - E(0)|/E(0)], E(0) = 3.75 J analytic. Declared rule: dt* is the largest grid dt such that all grid dt at or below it are stable with epsilon below EPS_THRESHOLD = 1e-3 (energy conserved to better than 0.1 percent on average), and not above the largest grid dt with tc/dt >= MIN_STEPS_PER_CONTACT = 50 (DEM practice: dt about tc/50, the minimum accepted is 10; research PITFALLS Pitfall 5). The threshold 1e-4 was rejected as a default because the planner's data put epsilon at 5e-5 between 9e-5 and 9.5e-5, a coin flip against the threshold that would swing the cost by 2.5 times. The group may change either constant in dt_star.py; that is a recorded decision followed by a re-run of `--replot` and `--check-frozen`.
- **PD-52 What is cached.** runs.csv (one row per run), summary.csv (one row per dt) and the 501-point series CSVs are the only inputs of `--replot`; the 12 MB frames files are inputs of the first pass only. All of these live under `data/{study}/`, whose subfolders encode every parameter.
- **PD-53 Divergence in the figure.** A dt whose runs diverge has no epsilon; it is drawn as a hollow upward triangle at the top of the axes (axes-fraction y = 0.96) with a legend entry in words, never dropped silently.
- **PD-54 Reversibility.** Freezing DT_STAR changes the cost and the name of every later run by up to tenfold and all later sweeps inherit it, but undoing it needs no migration, only a constant change and re-running the sweeps: rated `costly` on Task 3 (flagged, no checkpoint).
