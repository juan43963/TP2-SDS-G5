## Planner decisions (Claude's discretion — no CONTEXT.md for this phase)

- **PD-40 Drawing.** Wall as an unfilled black circle of radius R; obstacles as filled black circles of radius `radius` at (-x0, 0) and (+x0, 0) with a higher zorder than the particles (so a particle overlapping an obstacle never hides it); particles as one `EllipseCollection` in data units with `set_offsets` and `set_facecolor` per frame (fast enough for N = 650); the axes are equal-aspect with limits 1.03*R and ticks at -0.5, 0, 0.5; axis labels in words with MKS units through plot_style.axis_label; no figure title (guide 1.7); clock text of FONT_SIZE in the upper-left corner reading `t = {t:.2f} s`.
- **PD-41 Video format.** `--format auto` picks MP4 (FFMpegWriter, codec h264) when ffmpeg is available and otherwise GIF (PillowWriter) with a printed notice. GIF is a review artifact only; Phase 6 renders the MP4s for YouTube/Vimeo with ffmpeg from Windows `py`. A figure size of 8 x 8 inches at the default 100 dpi gives 800 x 800 px, an even size as H.264 requires.
- **PD-42 Frame selection.** `iter_selected` takes every `stride`-th frame of the stream and stops after `max_frames`; the PNG path streams the whole file once and keeps only the frame whose t is nearest to the requested time (default: header.tf / 2).
- **PD-43 Independence.** The module may import only the standard library, numpy, matplotlib, tp4io and plot_style. A test parses the source with `ast` and fails on any other project import.

- `class Scene`: `__init__(self, header, figsize=(8.0, 8.0))` calls plot_style.apply_style(), creates the figure and one axes, and adds: the wall (`matplotlib.patches.Circle((0, 0), header.R, fill=False, edgecolor=COLOR_WALL, linewidth=2)`) stored in `wall`; when header.obstacles is true two filled `Circle` patches of radius header.radius at (-header.x0, 0) and (+header.x0, 0), colour COLOR_OBSTACLE, zorder 3, stored in `obstacle_patches` (an empty list when there are no obstacles); the particles as one `EllipseCollection(widths=2*radius, heights=2*radius, angles=0, units="xy", offsets=zeros((N, 2)), offset_transform=ax.transData, facecolors=COLOR_FRESH, edgecolors="none", zorder=2)` stored in `particles` (no outline, so the blue and red pixel colours are exact); and the clock as an `ax.text` in axes coordinates (upper-left) with fontsize plot_style.FONT_SIZE stored in `clock`.
