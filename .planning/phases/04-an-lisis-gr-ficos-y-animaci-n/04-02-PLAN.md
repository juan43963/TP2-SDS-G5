<objective>
Build `TP2/python/animate.py`, the standalone animation module: it launches its own dedicated full-trajectory `tp2` runs (one per model, at rho=2, eta chosen inside that model's detected order-disorder transition bracket), parses the resulting position+velocity trajectory files, and renders each as a GIF with particles colored by heading angle via a cyclic colormap.
